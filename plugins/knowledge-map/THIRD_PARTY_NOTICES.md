# Third-party visual assets

The 3D canvas includes the following planetary texture maps:

- Moon: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data), public-domain U.S. government work. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
- Mars: Viking global texture processed by Caltech/JPL/USGS. Source: https://space.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/mars.html
- Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune: JPL/Caltech Solar System Simulator planetary texture maps, based on spacecraft imagery or representative visualizations as documented per body. Source: https://space.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/

The texture files are reduced-resolution copies packaged locally for offline rendering.
